# ADR authoring

Consult `docs/adr/README.md`, accepted decisions, and the [glossary](../../docs/glossary.md). Capture context, drivers, considered options, decision, consequences, and references. Distinguish a proposal from an accepted decision; only the human decision and project process may change ADR status. An ADR does not itself approve implementation.

Keep the decision scoped, link relevant evidence, use project terminology consistently, and avoid implying tooling or enforcement that does not exist.
