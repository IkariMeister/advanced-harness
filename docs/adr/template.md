## Context

The problem, the forces at play, and why a decision is needed now.

## Decision Drivers

1. The qualities the decision must preserve or improve.

## Considered Options

### A. Option name

What it does, its benefits and costs, and why it was chosen or rejected.

## Decision

The chosen option, stated so that conformance can be checked.

## Consequences

**Positive:** What gets better.

**Negative:** What gets worse or costs more.

**Neutral:** What changes without being better or worse.

## Adoption

Accepting this ADR does not change tooling, roles, permissions, or defaults. Describe what a separate approved implementation plan must cover, and how to roll it back.

## Reconsideration

The observable conditions, based on recorded outcomes, that would reopen this decision. It is also reviewed at each milestone close.
